Privacy Policy

Updated September 21, 2026

This policy describes what personal data is collected on the yaeldruckman.com website, for what purposes, and to whom it may be disclosed. It applies to this website in accordance with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741–1981, including Amendment 13.

Who Collects the Information

Yael Druckman, office in Herzliya, Israel.
Email: office@yaeldruckman.com
Phone: 058-6306460
Address: 51B Ha'atzmaut St., Herzliya, Israel

Contact Form

Through the form, you may provide your full name, phone number, email address, area of interest, and message. The sole purpose is to contact you regarding your inquiry. Providing these details is voluntary; without contact details, we will not be able to reply.

Form submissions are transmitted via Google Apps Script and delivered to office@yaeldruckman.com in Gmail. Google processes the message for transmission and secure email storage.

Before submission, Cloudflare Turnstile operates to verify that the submission originates from a human rather than an automated bot. For this purpose, Cloudflare receives technical browser parameters, including your IP address.

Message content may contain sensitive information (such as personal health, family, insurance, or legal matters). We recommend providing only what is strictly necessary to establish initial contact.

Office Map

The physical office address is displayed as plain text on the website. The interactive map is embedded via Google Maps. When your browser loads the map, it requests tiles from Google servers, and Google receives the visitor's IP address. This occurs solely to render the map tiles on your device, not for our office to track your location.

Additional Services & Components

  • Clicking a video on the homepage opens an embedded YouTube player hosted on Google servers.
  • Clicking WhatsApp redirects to the official WhatsApp application or web service (Meta).
  • This website does not use tracking or behavioral analytics tools such as Google Analytics.

Testimonials

Names and testimonials featured on the homepage are published with the prior explicit consent of the clients.

Data Retention and Usage

Inquiries are retained only as long as required to handle your matter and fulfill our professional and legal obligations. Personal data is never sold or shared for advertising, and is never used for marketing mailings unless explicitly requested.

Your Rights

You have the right under applicable law to review, amend, or request the deletion of your personal information. To do so, please contact office@yaeldruckman.com. You may also address inquiries to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.
