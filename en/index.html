About

Experience, knowledge, and personal care that make the difference

A graduate of Reichman University in Law and Business Administration (2004). Admitted to practice law in 2006, licensed as an insurance agent in 2008, and qualified as a mediator in 2022.

For more than twenty years, I have supported individuals, families, self-employed professionals, and business owners through different stages of life and changing needs. I combine knowledge and practical experience in law, insurance, and finance, helping people secure the rights and benefits they are entitled to in everyday life. My personal journey, having lost my mother at the age of fifteen, led me to choose this path—guided by purpose, integrity, justice, and care for others.
