Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Updated September 21, 2026

The website yaeldruckman.com provides information regarding the practice and enables visitors to contact the office. Our target conformance level is Israeli Standard 5568 (which adopts the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines WCAG 2.0 at Level AA), pursuant to the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Accessibility Adjustments for Services), 5773–2013.

Accessibility Features Implemented

  • Skip link leading directly to the primary content area
  • Descriptive programmatic labels for all form input fields
  • Alternative text for informative graphic elements and photos
  • Full keyboard navigability across primary and mobile navigation
  • Logical heading structure and responsive design adapting to any screen size

Third-Party Exceptions

  • The interactive Google Maps component is a third-party service; its accessibility is maintained by Google.
  • The media interview clip from Channel 10 is hosted on YouTube; separate standalone subtitles are not embedded directly on this site.

Reporting an Accessibility Issue

If you encounter an accessibility hurdle on this website or require assistance, please email or call us. We will make every effort to address and resolve the issue promptly.
